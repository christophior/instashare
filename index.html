instashare

Share your photographs to Instagram, straight from your Mac.

Archived project

This project is no longer maintained

instashare was a small macOS app I built in 2019, back when posting to Instagram from a desktop meant reaching for your phone. It is no longer developed, supported, or available for purchase.

The page is kept online as a record of the project. If you landed here looking for a download, there isn’t one — Instagram now supports posting from the web directly.
